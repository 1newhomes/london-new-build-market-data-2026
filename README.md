# 1newhomes London — UK New Build Property & Market Data

**[1newhomes.com](https://1newhomes.com)** is an independent residential property platform and market research hub tracking **1,635+ off-plan and newly completed residential developments** across Greater London (Zones 1 to 9).

---

## 🔗 Key Platform Hubs & Interactive Tools

* 🏙️ **[London New Build Developments Catalog](https://1newhomes.com/new-homes/)** — Browse active residential schemes by London borough, postcode, delivery year, and homebuilder.
* 🏢 **[New Build Flats & Apartments in London](https://1newhomes.com/flats/)** — Studio, 1, 2, 3, and 4-bedroom apartments and penthouses with verified floorplans and starting prices.
* 🗺️ **[Interactive London New Builds Map](https://1newhomes.com/new-homes/map)** — Geolocation search across Tube, Elizabeth Line, DLR, and Overground stations.
* 🏗️ **[UK Property Developers Directory](https://1newhomes.com/developers)** — Track records and active London portfolios for 520+ UK homebuilders including [Barratt London](https://1newhomes.com/barratt-london/), [Taylor Wimpey](https://1newhomes.com/taylor-wimpey-london/), [Mount Anvil](https://1newhomes.com/mount-anvil-london/), and [Bellway](https://1newhomes.com/bellway-london/).
* 📍 **[London Neighbourhood Guides](https://1newhomes.com/neighbourhood/)** — Area profiles for [Battersea](https://1newhomes.com/neighbourhood/battersea), [Canary Wharf](https://1newhomes.com/neighbourhood/canary-wharf), [Greenwich](https://1newhomes.com/neighbourhood/greenwich), [Stratford](https://1newhomes.com/neighbourhood/stratford), and 30+ London districts.
